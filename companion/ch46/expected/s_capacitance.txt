capacitance of a core from its geometry,
  C = 2 pi eps0 eps_r / ln(ri/rc), eps_r 2.3
  size mm2   C uF/km (geometry)   C uF/km (ch 24 data)   charging A/km
       240                0.218                   0.21            1.30
       400                0.262                   0.25            1.57
       630                0.311                   0.30            1.86

  thinned to 7.1 mm all round would show 0.239 uF/km (+10%) -
