# Chapter 22 plan: Feasibility and option selection (budget 60 pp; process chapter)

still be preferred); blueprint A-G; audit row 22 (theory: feasibility, social safeguards, investment
gates, decision theory; applied: compare sites/options, social-risk evidence, financeability;

bankability sensitivities; manufacturing: supply-chain capacity, labour exposure, buildability

community, people or place; social safeguards screened, not judged (specialists and affected people
decide); no legal conclusions (local counsel); resettlement costs teaching values only.
