# Chapter 31 plan: Transport, lifting and installation logistics (budget 55 pp; process chapter)

Handoff: 3 bullets + worked example (plan delivery and lifting of a major component with weather and
access hold points, emergency access and required approvals); blueprint A-E; audit row 31
(logistics, lifting, preservation and export-system installation; route major plant/export components;
plan receipt, storage, delivery, workface sequence and lift interfaces; calculate route/handling
envelopes from approved data; trace packaging, preservation, transport and installation readiness;
deliver a logistics plan with hold points and qualified approvals).

Safeguards: a planning exercise, never an approved lift plan; crane capacities from the crane's own
load charts and the lift engineer; no tactical detail; rescue and medical by qualified responders;
permits and route rules are the authority's (placeholders U-106); fictional route and port.

## Data (A-363..A-367)
Components of the WT-5.0-150 (ch 11 masses, ch 30 tower sections), main transformer 95 t / 72 t
transport (ch 25), cable drums (ch 24), conductor reels; a fictional port-to-site route with bridges,
turns and a low wire; a teaching crane load chart; site wind from the data pack; laydown areas.
