# Chapter 32 plan: Occupational safety, fire protection and emergency response (budget 100 pp)

Handoff: 9 bullets + three worked items (fire-hazard register and concept protection matrix with a
sourced firewater/autonomy or response-coverage case and detection-to-action logic; job hazard
analysis for a maintenance task with residual risk; synthetic remote-site rescue and access-control
review without tactical procedures); blueprint A-H; audit row 32.

Safeguards (binding): no live-work or energised procedures - isolation and permits taught by purpose,
roles and evidence; no universal fire-system prescriptions - every rate, duration, separation and
suppression choice comes from the project's fire strategy, the authority having jurisdiction and
product tests (placeholders, U-105); physical security risk-based, rights-respecting and
non-tactical; rescue and medical by qualified providers; legal obligations kept apart from project
and lender commitments; no country requirement invented; fictional site and people.

## Data (A-368..A-375)
Synthetic incident and hour records, a teaching 5 x 5 risk matrix, Plant P areas and equipment
(ch 18-31), fire scenarios by area, placeholder detection and response times, the ch 29 tank and
reserve, ch 31 response times, data-pack temperatures for heat stress, workforce numbers (ch 29).
