# Chapter 47 plan: civil, structural, tower, tracker and balance-of-plant manufacture (budget 75 pp, plan for ~85)

Handoff: 6 bullets (design foundations; PV mounting and trackers; tower and steelwork production;
foundations and concrete; factory/site distinction; worked examples - material take-off and
simplified load path, review a tower fabrication dossier, foundation inspection/quality plan from
an assumed geotechnical basis with the professional approvals listed). Blueprint A-D (A design
inputs and product families; B fabrication and production; C inspection and field integration;
D quality dossier lab). Audit row 47.

Running products: the 100 m steel tower of ch 11/30 (four sections, 340 t), the turbine
foundation of ch 29/30 (r 10 m, 620 m3), the fixed PV tables of D-013 (2P x 26, 25 deg) and a
single-axis tracker variant for comparison, the substation steel and the containers' plinths;
a fictional tower fabricator, a fictional tracker maker and a fictional ready-mix supplier.

Overlap rule: ch 29 covers ground, roads, concrete strength (characteristic value), hot
concrete, mass pours, ITP of a foundation and the civil take-off; ch 30 covers loads, the
tower's frequency and fatigue, the traveler, weld window, UT, DFT, dimensions, plates,
bolting, rebar, maturity, p-chart, stack-up, preheat, piles. Ch 47 goes to the records and the
dossier: mill certificates checked against the order (grade, toughness temperature,
chemistry, CEV), heat-number genealogy plate -> can -> section, welder and procedure
qualification matrix with expiry, weld map and NDT coverage against the plan, flange
flatness and ovality records, galvanizing mass from section thickness, fastener lots,
tracker assembly (torque tube straightness, slew drive and bearing records, actuator
end-of-line), bar bending schedule validator (bar marks, shapes, kg/m = d^2/162), batching
tickets checker (time, water added, temperature), cube conformity read with the order,
curing record, anchor-cage survey, NCR register ageing, tower dossier review with planted
gaps, material take-off and load path, foundation inspection and quality plan with the
approvals of structural and geotechnical professionals.

Safeguards: no welding parameters to apply, no heat-treatment or cure schedules, no concrete
mix recipe; structural/geotechnical decisions named as the licensed engineers' (no design
approval implied); teaching values only.
