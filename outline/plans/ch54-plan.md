# Chapter 54 plan: how to identify applicable requirements (budget 45 pp, plan for ~55)

Handoff: 7 bullets (law, permits, national standards, grid codes, utility rules, contracts, owner
specifications distinguished; responsible authorities and utilities for the actual location,
voltage and project; official source, edition, amendments, language, adoption/legal status and
applicability verified; conflicts, interpretations, required evidence, approval responsibility and
revision history recorded; a living compliance register with written clarification of ambiguity;
finance and fiscal sources (tax, incentives, currency, repatriation, ownership, lending, security,
insurance) verified from current authoritative sources and qualified local advisors, never
assumed; worked example: one hypothetical grid-connection requirement traced from official source
through design, test evidence, reviewer and closure). Blueprint A-E (A requirement hierarchy, B
research and applicability method, C compliance evidence management, D traceability exercise, E
finance and fiscal source verification). Audit row 54. Persian letters A-E = الف ب پ ت ث.

Jurisdiction: a FICTIONAL country ("Country Z", «کشورِ Z») with fictional authorities, documents
and numbers, so the method is taught without stating any real country's rule. Chapters 55-57 apply
the method to Iran, Afghanistan and Tajikistan from live research; ch 58 treats international
standards.

Running data: Plant P's existing requirement registers (ch 17 REQ-001..012, ch 28 REQ-G01..G24,
ch 38 RVTM), the ch 50 grid-test record (Q at 100 MW 30.6 vs 33 Mvar = DEV-11), the ch 21 finance
terms (as teaching values only).

Safeguards: no real country requirement stated as fact; every requirement fictional and labelled;
no legal, tax or financial advice; statuses verified / project-dependent / to-confirm.
