# Chapter 59 plan - Capstone: design, deliver and operate Plant P (110 pp minimum)

Handoff: the 14-item staged dossier, the final defense exercise, blueprint A-J, audit row 59.
The chapter compiles; it does not re-teach. Every number is computed by calling the earlier
chapters' models (ch 15 yield, ch 16 uncertainty, ch 18 architecture, ch 20 control, ch 21 finance,
ch 22 appraisal, ch 28 grid study, ch 33 E&S, ch 38 document control, ch 52 RAM, ch 53 lifecycle)
or read from their tested outputs, with the source named.

## Section files (Persian letters A-J = الف ب پ ت ث ج چ ح خ د)
- map: objectives, pre-test, how to use the chapter; the 14-item dossier index (computed:
  decisions, documents, chapters, open unknowns per item; completeness check).
- A basis and feasibility: requirements baseline, assumptions ledger by status, resource and data
  quality, site and water balance, yield and uncertainty (P50/P90 recomputed), economics, risks,
  decision gates (gate scorecard).
- B integrated design: architecture and equipment basis table, one-line and auxiliaries, civil and
  structures, controls/comms/cyber, safety/fire/environment, grid-study results, compliance; a
  design-interface check across chapters (numbers that must agree: export limit, transformer
  rating, ride-through, reactive range, battery size).
- C delivery and lifecycle: procurement packages, manufacturing quality gates, construction and
  commissioning schedule, handover, operations, maintenance, repowering and retirement;
  plant-owned collector/export scope vs utility works.
- D defense and change: decision-by-decision defense table (evidence, uncertainty, approver);
  four change cases recomputed (wind resource -10 %, export limit 80 MW, capex +15 %, a turbine
  change) through yield, finance and design consequences.
- E reliability case: availability allocation, critical-equipment ranking, common-cause, data
  needed, Bayesian update of failure rates with evidence (gamma-Poisson), effect on energy.
- F forecasting and grid services: reproducible forecast validation on the synthetic archive by
  horizon, quantile coverage, forecast to dispatch/reserve, service envelope, requirement-to-
  model/test matrix, skill vs capability vs contract vs delivered.
- G grid connection and export: scope/ownership matrix, route/corridor register, export options
  lifecycle cost/risk, POC interface, study/protection inputs, evidence plan, O&M boundary.
- H project finance: base/downside/break-even, lenders' diligence and CP registers, contract-risk
  allocation, financeability recommendation.
- I land and social safeguards: baseline, rights and livelihood, mitigation, consultation,
  grievance, contractor controls, monitoring KPIs, budget, closure evidence.
- J asset-information handover: information requirements, hierarchy, linked records, schema and
  cross-reference validation, CMMS import, change/repair and retirement/migration scenarios.
- y: final defense exercise, country appendices pointer (ch 55-57), D-063, deliverables.
- z: summary, vocabulary, five-level assessment (100+ problems), solutions, references.
