## 1 Institutions
1.1 Ministry of Energy (وزارت نیرو): legal mandate to buy renewable electricity through "the relevant
    organisation" under long-term guaranteed-purchase contracts. VERIFIED (Law on Reform of Energy
    Consumption Pattern, Art 61, http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/ira164435.pdf; 2016 print). Website
    www.moe.gov.ir unreachable; MoE single window https://my.moe.gov.ir/ (linked from wrm.ir).
1.2 SATBA (سازمان انرژی‌های تجدیدپذیر و بهره‌وری انرژی برق) is the Art 61 counterparty. VERIFIED (Cabinet
    bylaw of Art 14 of the Law on Removing Obstacles to Electricity Industry Development, No.
    142419/ت61198هـ, 1402/8/10, Art 1, http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/ira235384.pdf). The Nov 2024
    amendment writes «سازمان انرژیهای تجدیدپذیر و بهرهوری ایران»: current official name TO-CONFIRM. Merger
    of SUNA and SABA (1395/9/24) SECONDARY.
1.3 Tavanir (شرکت مادرتخصصی توانیر; site title «شرکت سهامی مدیریت تولید، انتقال و توزیع نیروی برق ایران")
    announces transmission tariffs and develops the upstream network. VERIFIED (Law 1401 Art 17 note,
    ira218000.pdf; Art 4 bylaw Art 3(d), ira235382.pdf; archived site 2021).
1.4 Iran Grid Management Company (شرکت مدیریت شبکه برق ایران, IGMC) issues standards for grid stability;
    all grid-connected plants must comply; coordinates delivery of renewable output. VERIFIED (Art 4 bylaw
    No. 146796/ت61218هـ, 1402/8/16, Art 6, http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/ira235382.pdf; Art 61 Note 2).
1.5 Regional electricity companies and distribution companies must take delivery of renewable electricity
    and buy it from the organisation. VERIFIED (Art 61 Note 2). Regional company CEOs now issue permits
    and contracts with provincial governorates: SECONDARY (Mehr, 5 Apr 2026,
    https://www.mehrnews.com/news/6792453).
1.6 Electricity market regulatory board (هیئت تنظیم بازار برق): no official text found. TO-CONFIRM.
1.7 Iran Energy Exchange (شرکت بورس انرژی ایران): venue for renewable fuel-credit drafts and the "green
    electricity board" (تابلوی برق سبز). VERIFIED (Art 14 bylaw Art 6 note; amendment Art 10(b),
    ira235389.pdf).
1.8 Department of Environment (سازمان حفاظت محیط زیست) is the EIA authority. VERIFIED (EIA bylaw, 5.1);
    live site unreachable.
1.9 Iranian National Standards Organization (سازمان ملی استاندارد ایران, formerly ISIRI).
    VERIFIED (https://www.inso.gov.ir/, https://en.inso.gov.ir/).
1.10 Iran Water Resources Management Company (شرکت مدیریت منابع آب ایران). VERIFIED (https://www.wrm.ir/).
     Which regional water company and role: TO-CONFIRM.
1.11 Natural Resources and Watershed Management Organization (سازمان منابع طبیعی و آبخیزداری کشور)
     allocates national land for renewable plants; Ministry of Agriculture Jihad and Land Affairs
     Organization (سازمان امور اراضی کشور) oversee. VERIFIED (Renewables Bylaw Art 3, ira235386.pdf;
     land directive 7.3; https://frw.ir/fa).
1.12 Civil aviation obstacle rule for wind turbines: not found; the Renewables Bylaw setbacks do not
     mention aviation. TO-CONFIRM (cao.ir 403).
1.13 Applicant must respect legal setbacks for gas, electricity, roads, cultural heritage and passive
     defence. VERIFIED (Renewables Bylaw Art 3). Heritage ministry procedure TO-CONFIRM.
1.14 Labour Code 1990, Chapter IV, technical protection and occupational health; regulations 1996.
     SECONDARY (ILO NATLEX).
1.15 Organization for Investment, Economic and Technical Assistance of Iran: sole body for foreign
     investment. SECONDARY (FIPPA Art 5, UNCTAD unofficial translation,
     http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/ira202448E.pdf).

## 2 Licensing and permitting
2.1 Guaranteed-purchase price and conditions set on the MoE's proposal with Cabinet approval. VERIFIED
    (Art 61 Note 1, Law of 1389/12/4). Art 61 executive bylaw No. 153440/ت52375هـ of 1394/11/21 (not read).
2.2 «آیین‌نامه رفع موانع احداث نیروگاه‌های تجدیدپذیر», Cabinet No. 238700/ت62054هـ, 1402/12/23 (13 Mar 2024):
    land, DOE inquiry, other permits; Art 6: valid two years from entry into force. VERIFIED
    (http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/ira235386.pdf). May have lapsed ~March 2026; extension TO-CONFIRM.
2.3 Amended by No. 134137/ت63365هـ, 1403/9/4 (24 Nov 2024), Official Gazette No. 23237 of 12 Dey 1403;
    basis Art 46(a) of the Seventh Development Plan Law (1403); aim 30,000 MW; Art 12: MoE to draft a
    "renewable generation regulatory framework" within two months for Economic Council approval.
    VERIFIED (http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/ira235389.pdf). Framework adoption TO-CONFIRM.
2.4 SATBA construction permit (پروانه احداث) required; permitting, land and connection devolved to the
    provinces from the second half of 1404. SECONDARY (Taadol 7 Dec 2025; Mehr 20 Jul 2026).
2.5 Economic Council authorised 8,000 MW of wind and solar with National Development Fund financing up to
    80 %; SATBA confirms technical competence; Tavanir signs long-term transmission contracts. SECONDARY
    (Mehr 21 Sep 2025); no decision number.

## 3 Grid connection
3.1 For energy-intensive industries' plants: adjacent substation and immediate line are the developer's;
    upstream network the MoE's (Tavanir). VERIFIED rule, PROJECT-DEPENDENT (Art 4 bylaw Art 3(d)).
    No general rule for merchant plants found.
3.2 NDF scheme: MoE commitment letters for new or expanded 63, 230 and 400 kV substations. SECONDARY. No
    source mentions 132 kV: TO-CONFIRM / PROJECT-DEPENDENT.
3.3 MoE must allow transmission lines to be used for sale or export up to network capacity; transmission
    or balancing fees prohibited, subject to the Law on Financing of Production and Infrastructure (1402).
    VERIFIED (amendment Art 10(a), (c)); applies to plants under the bylaw.
3.4 No official grid code or wind/PV technical connection document found (reactive power, FRT,
    frequency). TO-CONFIRM. IGMC reportedly drafts requirements with SATBA and Tavanir; Tavanir reviews
    plans within two weeks: SECONDARY (Taadol).
3.5 "Visibility" (رویت‌پذیری) to the grid control centre after meter sealing and connection; SATBA reported
    71 % visible. SECONDARY (Tejarat News/ISNA 12 May 2026). Metering and SCADA specs TO-CONFIRM.

## 4 Offtake, tariff, settlement
4.1 Government budgets the value of fuel saved by renewables and pays Art 61 contract holders in crude-oil
    equivalent "energy drafts" (حواله سبد سوخت); SATBA reports quarterly; drafts tradable on the Energy
    Exchange; FX conversion at the Central Bank ETS average rate. VERIFIED (Law on Removing Obstacles to
    Electricity Industry Development, 1401/8/15, Art 14, ira218000.pdf; Art 14 bylaw ira235384.pdf).
4.2 Under the amended bylaw (except contracts already signed) output belongs to the investor for the
    plant's life; mandated purchase or sale prices prohibited; export (except at peak), green board, or
    bilateral contracts with industries. VERIFIED (amendment Arts 9-10); implementation TO-CONFIRM.
4.3 MoE must issue electricity-export licences to the private sector, especially renewables, once domestic
    supply is secured; energy-intensive industries to build 1,000 MW by end of 1404. VERIFIED (Law 1401
    Arts 4, 17).
4.4 MoE decision of 10 Farvardin 1405 raised guaranteed tariffs for small solar (up to 1 MW). SECONDARY;
    not shown to apply to 100 MW plants.
4.5 Consumers >= 150 kW buy through the exchange or licensed retailers; green-board electricity exempt from
    summer curtailment. SECONDARY (bylaw of Seventh Plan Art 43(b) as reported).
4.6 Utility-scale PPA term, currency, indexation, settlement, payment security: not established.
    TO-CONFIRM.

## 5 Environmental approval
5.1 New EIA bylaw Cabinet No. 17882/ت63794هـ, 1405/03/04 (25 May 2026), under Seventh Plan Art 22(a); annex
    lists "wind, solar 300 MW and above" as subject to EIA; listed projects need a DOE assessment permit
    before works; in force three months after notification; replaces 214287/ت45880هـ (1390/11/3).
    VERIFIED (http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/ira248720.pdf, scanned official text). 100 MW below the
    threshold; protected areas PROJECT-DEPENDENT.
5.2 Wind and solar above 100 MW excluded from category 7 of the industrial siting rules and not subject to
    EIA studies; after land allocation only a DOE inquiry, answered within one week; under the amendment
    silence counts as consent. VERIFIED (Renewables Bylaw Arts 3, 5; amendment item 2). Time-limited (2.2).

## 7 Land
7.1 National and state land may be allocated to Iranian natural or legal persons for wind and solar:
    SATBA confirms eligibility and performance guarantee; land returned if nothing done within one year;
    applicant pays compensation and value of improvements (Art 33, Law on Protection and Exploitation of
    Forests and Rangelands); annual lease capped at 20 % of regional land value; no provincial working
    group or Roads ministry inquiry needed. VERIFIED (Renewables Bylaw Arts 1, 2, 4). Foreign eligibility
    TO-CONFIRM; validity as 2.2.
7.2 SATBA identifies suitable areas and requests allocation and land-use change; approved applicants may
    apply directly to the Ministry of Agriculture Jihad. VERIFIED (amendment new Art 2).
7.3 "Revised directive on allocation of national and state lands for agricultural and non-agricultural
    projects", Ministry of Agriculture Jihad No. 020/184659, 1403/7/29 (20 Oct 2024), 41 articles.
    VERIFIED (header only; http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/ira235392.pdf).

## 8 Standards

    5 kW" (HS 85044086) and power/instrument transformers (HS 8504.21-8504.32); no PV-module (HS 8541) or
    wind generating set entry. VERIFIED
    (https://qa.inso.gov.ir/portal/file/?1151613/فهرست-واردات-آبان-1405.pdf). Large inverters TO-CONFIRM.
8.2 INSO mandatory national standards list: no PV-module, PV-inverter or wind-turbine entries; includes
    power transformers (INSO 2620-1), MV cables 1-30 kV (INSO 3569-1/-2), stationary VRLA batteries
    (4868-22), lithium secondary cells (6618-3). VERIFIED
    (https://nezarat.inso.gov.ir/portal/file/?1127643/فهرست-استانداردهاي-اجباري.xlsx, modified 30 Jun 2026).
8.3 National standards adopting IEC 61215, 61730, 62109, 61400: not found. TO-CONFIRM.
8.4 MoE sets criteria and standards for renewable equipment, priority to domestic supply. VERIFIED
    (amendment Art 7(a)).
