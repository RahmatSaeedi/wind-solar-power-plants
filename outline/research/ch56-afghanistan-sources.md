# Chapter 56 source research: Afghanistan (accessed 2026-11-17)

Research agent report, kept as the source register draft for chapter 56. Every item still needs the
author's spot-check before it is printed as تأییدشده. DPMEA = Office of the Deputy Prime Minister for
Economic Affairs (dpmea.gov.af, official de facto authority site); OG = Official Gazette. World Bank,
OSCE, GIZ, ISO and news items are SECONDARY.

Caveat for every pre-2021 instrument: EUAA COI report "Afghanistan: Country Focus" (Feb 2026,
https://www.euaa.europa.eu/coi-report-afghanistan-country-focus/122-nature-issued-instructions) states
that all laws of the previous government "have been undergoing a review to assess their compliance with
sharia" and that "no announcement has been made on the final outcome... or which laws remain in effect"
(SECONDARY). Current applicability of each pre-2021 law, policy or regulation: TO-CONFIRM, even where
its text is VERIFIED.

## 2 Legal framework
2.1 Electricity law «قانون تنظیم خدمات انرژی برق» (MEW English "Power Services Regulation Act"):
    legislative decree on Cabinet Approval No. 19 (1394/2015), 12 chapters, 53 articles, published after
    National Assembly approval in OG No. 1231, 27 Mizan 1395 (18 Oct 2016). VERIFIED (OG PDF on MEW
    "Policies and Laws" page). Date 2015/2016, not 2019/2020.
2.2 Art 8: no construction, generation, transmission, import or export without a licence and a contract
    with MEW (self-use and rural supply up to 100 kW exempt); Art 9: Afghan or foreign legal entity with
    investment licence, financial guarantee, foreign entities keep a permanent agency; Art 10: five
    licence types, fees and guarantees set by government on MEW proposal; Art 11: generation licence at
    most 25 years. VERIFIED text; current use TO-CONFIRM.
2.3 Art 19 MEW leases state land; Art 21 private land leased by agreement or acquired by MEW; Art 23
    licensee prepares resettlement plan "in consultation with" affected people and funds fair
    replacement; Art 36 respect historical, cultural, archaeological, environmental sites; Art 41 disputes:
    MEW Disputes Board, chamber of commerce centre, ICSID, UNCITRAL, Afghan courts; Art 40 foreign
    investment per private investment law; Art 50 documents in English plus Dari or Pashto. VERIFIED text;
    enforceability TO-CONFIRM.
2.4 Renewable Energy Policy (MEW 2015), 2015-2032 in two phases; RER2032 (MEW/ADB TA-8808, June 2017)
    states "4500-5000 MW of renewable energy capacity by 2032"; other summaries conflict. SECONDARY
    (policy PDF not opened). Current status TO-CONFIRM.
2.5 PPP Law 2016 (OG 1228) reported replaced by PPP Law 2018 (OG 1322, 5 Sep 2018), amended 2020; Central
    Partnership Authority in Ministry of Finance. SECONDARY; post-2021 status TO-CONFIRM.
2.6 Law on Domestic and Foreign Private Investment (OG 869, 21 Dec 2005); Feb 2026 Economic Commission:
    1-10-year residence permits for foreign investors. SECONDARY.
2.7 Land Acquisition (Expropriation) Law OG 1258, 4 Mar 2017. SECONDARY; status TO-CONFIRM; 2026 reports
    of mass "state land" declarations (KabulNow Aug 2026): land title risk high.

## 4 Grid
4.1 No unified national grid: NEPS fed from Uzbekistan and Tajikistan; Herat zone from Iran and
    Turkmenistan; Kandahar unconnected to the central grid; imports 220 kV Uzbekistan (2009), 220 kV
    Tajikistan (2011), 500 kV Kerki-Shibirghan operated at 220 kV; 110 kV Serhetabat-Herat upgraded 2021.
    SECONDARY (OSCE 2024, https://www.osce.org/files/f/documents/5/e/564931.pdf).
4.2 220 kV and 110 kV in use: MEW ICB tender MOWE/1405/ICB/W-3184 (7 Nov 2026, closing 7 Dec 2026) design-
    build 220 kV double-circuit Taloqan-Keshem; 110 kV Kajaki-Kandahar extension (Mar 2025); rehabilitated
    Maimana-Andkhoy 110 kV commissioned 14 Nov 2026. VERIFIED. No Afghan source mentions 132 kV.
4.3 DABS chief Aug 2025: domestic generation ~250 MW, imports ~800 MW, USD 250-280 m a year. SECONDARY.
4.4 Law: distribution = MV and LV, exceptionally HV up to 110 kV; dominant licensee publishes connection
    points for comment, answers requests within 40 business days; disputes to the regulation department
    (Arts 3, 32-34). VERIFIED text.
4.5 No published grid code, distribution code, VRE connection, metering or telemetry rules found.
    TO-CONFIRM. March 2024 bid for DABS central dispatch upgrade reported (page not opened).
4.6 75 % of windy area and 90 % of exploitable wind in Herat, Farah, Nimroz; Herat isolated, Farah no grid.
    SECONDARY (World Bank 2018).
4.7 World Bank: Afghan CASA-1000 activities resume "ring-fenced", payments outside Afghanistan.
    SECONDARY (worldbank.org/afghanistan, 1 Sep 2025).

## 5 Water
5.1 Water Affairs Management Law «قانون تنظیم امور آب» OG No. 1367, 4 Feb 2020, repeals the 2009 Water Law.
    VERIFIED (OG PDF on MEW page). Status TO-CONFIRM.
5.2 MEW "Guideline for Water Usage Permit" (posted March 2026): General Directorate of Water Affairs
    Regulation issues permits incl. industrial/commercial; apply to the sub-river-basin department;
    committee surveys technical, legal, social issues; MEW Kabul agrees; conditions: sufficient surface
    water, positive groundwater balance, no harm to residents; meter on wells; no use beyond allocation;
    fee "None". VERIFIED (https://mew.gov.af/sites/default/files/2026-03/...pdf). No power-plant category.
