## 2 Legal framework
13. Law on Energy No. 33 (2000, to 2013, and 2022): licensing per licensing legislation; mandatory state
    expertise for large energy projects; 2022 state-support provisions. VERIFIED*. New Energy Law ordered
    Jan 2025 - none adopted found (TO-CONFIRM).
14. RES Law No. 587 (2010) Art 11: network operators connect RES producers by contract if reliability is
    not harmed, "on a preferential basis"; must purchase RES energy under contract. VERIFIED*.
15. RES Law Arts 12, 17, 18: RES tariffs by the natural-monopoly regulator; certificate of conformity for
    energy sold; mandatory certification of RES energy and products. VERIFIED* (2010 text).
16. [R449] (01.07.2026, shown in force on Spinform): connection, metering, single-buyer model, net billing
    > 100 kW, auctions > 1 MW; overrides conflicting earlier acts. VERIFIED. Publication date unknown.
17. RES Programme 2023-2027 (Res. No. 51 of 01.03.2023), amended by Res. No. 221 of 28.04.2026. SECONDARY.
    2026-27 plan small (~6 MW solar + 10 MW floating-solar feasibility at Sarband).
18. Electric Power Sector Development Programme 2026-2030 (25.11.2025): 1,500 MW solar, 2,680 MW new hydro,
    losses 3 % / 9 %, 5 TWh exports. SECONDARY; resolution number TO-CONFIRM; another outlet 750 MW solar.
19. EBRD: government aim 1.5-3 GW wind and solar by 2030. SECONDARY (EBRD project 24449).
20. Investment framework listed in [RFQ]: Investments Law No. 1299 (2016), Investment Agreement Law
    No. 944 (2013), PPP Law No. 907 (2012), International Commercial Arbitration Law No. 1183 (2015),
    Tax Code (23.12.2021), Land Code (13.12.1996), Urban Planning Code (2012). VERIFIED as listed, not read.
21. Energy Saving Law amendment No. 1911 (19.07.2022): positive state energy-expertise conclusion
    mandatory before design approval, incl. electricity sources. VERIFIED*; issuing body TO-CONFIRM.
22. Environmental Code No. 2299 (21.10.2026) adopted; Spinform shows it repealing Law No. 760 (2011).
    SECONDARY; entry into force TO-CONFIRM.

## 3 Generation authorisation and PPAs
23. Permit System Law No. 1968 (in force 01.09.2023) repealed the 2004 Licensing Law; Unified Register;
    17.12.2025 amendment 208 -> 164 permits. VERIFIED* / SECONDARY. Whether generation needs a licence:
    TO-CONFIRM.
24. > 1 MW: price by competitive procedure; PPAs >= 15 years with the single buyer; network companies must
    connect. VERIFIED ([R449] items 7, 34). Single buyer not designated (PROJECT-DEPENDENT).
25. Bilateral PPAs only to isolated loads from small/medium plants; otherwise via single buyer. VERIFIED.
26. Sughd 200 MW tender: 25-year PPA with Barqi Tojik; USD-fixed tariff paid in somoni; Government
    Investment Agreement; BOO(T); evacuation assets transferred to SIB at COD; land leased from Barqi
    Tojik. VERIFIED ([RFQ] 2.4). Award TO-CONFIRM (shortlist ACWA Power, EDF, Universal Energy).
27. Investment Agreement with Ayon Energy LLC, 13.01.2026, two 250 MW solar plants (Asht, Sughd; Jayhun,
    Khatlon), USD 250 m. SECONDARY (Khovar).
28. Asht first kWh Aug 2026; TGEM EPC at Jayhun. SECONDARY (Interfax).
29. EBRD REMA: auctions for up to two ~200 MW solar projects. SECONDARY.
30. Masdar JV agreement with MEWR Oct 2023 to explore >= 500 MW. SECONDARY; no PPA found.

## 4 Grid
31. Transmission 500/220/110 kV; distribution 35 kV and below (110 kV in Dushanbe). SECONDARY ([GC18]).
32. Installed > 6,700 MW; 2025 generation 24 TWh. SECONDARY (Interfax 12.11.2026).
33. Hydro ~98 % of energy; output falls in autumn and winter. SECONDARY.
34. Dec 2025 imports from Uzbekistan over 500 kV Regar-Guzar. SECONDARY.
35. South-west reconnected to CAPS June 2024; north expected H1 2026. SECONDARY; completion TO-CONFIRM.
36. CASA-1000: Tajik and Kyrgyz works mainly complete; Afghan section by spring 2027; 1,300 MW summer
    export. SECONDARY.
37. No officially adopted grid code found; 2018 Grid Code is an ADB consultant draft. TO-CONFIRM.
38. [R449] items 14-19: technical conditions free within 30 working days; synchronous at 50 Hz with
    anti-islanding and frequency/voltage tripping; pf control and export limitation "recommended"; PV
    equipment on the Tier-1 list. No FRT or reactive values. VERIFIED.
39. [R449] items 20-21, 39-41: > 100 kW protection telesignalling and remote tripping; > 1 MW mandatory
    SCADA, real-time data, remote curtailment, hourly forecasts, 24/7 communications. VERIFIED.
40. Metering > 15 kW: class 1.0 or 0.5S at the ownership boundary, ASKUE, State Register, PUE. VERIFIED.
41. Curtailment allowed for stability, oversupply, emergencies; rules say not a buyer breach, but model
    PPA compensates lost profit beyond permitted values - internal inconsistency. VERIFIED.
